# The New Testament
### Introduction
This project is a devotional and linguistic exploration of the New Testament, translated directly from the Greek. It is not a scholarly edition, but a personal effort to deepen my understanding of the Word through study, reflection, and prayer. Each section includes notes on vocabulary, grammar, and interpretive choices.  I will follow up this work with a hand-copied final version.

Notes are organized according to the canonical order of the Catholic Bible to support an ongoing rhythm of reflection, devotion, and growth in the life of faith.
