# Matthew: Chapter 1, Verse 24

## ✍️ Greek Text (Base)
> ἐγερθεὶς δὲ ὁ Ἰωσὴφ ἀπὸ τοῦ ὕπνου ἐποίησεν ὡς προσέταξεν αὐτῷ ὁ ἄγγελος κυρίου καὶ παρέλαβεν τὴν γυναῖκα αὐτοῦ·

---

## 🧾 Vocabulary & Parsing Notes
>   - ἐγένετο – aorist middle of γίνομαι; "it happened"
>   - ἄγγελος – noun, masc., sing., nom.; can mean *messenger* or *angel*


---

## 📜 Translation Draft 
>   24. Then Joseph awoke from sleep, and did as the angel of the Lord enjoined him and took to himself his wife.


---

## 📚 Comparison Notes
> - NABRE:  When Joseph awoke, he did as the angel of the Lord had commanded him and took his wife into his home.
> - NJB:  When Joseph woke up he did what the angel of the Lord had told him to do: he took his wife to his home;
> - KJV:  Then Joseph being raised from sleep did as the angel of the Lord had bidden him, and took unto him his wife:
