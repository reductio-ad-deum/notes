# About RaD
----
Reductio ad Deum is a Catholic writer exploring the intersection of faith, reason, and tradition. His work on Catholicism reflects a personal journey deeper into the Church’s intellectual and spiritual heritage. RaD is was fallen-away for many years, and is reconnecting with the Catholic Church, with a special interest in liturgy, spiritual exercises and development, and the challenges of modern life.
