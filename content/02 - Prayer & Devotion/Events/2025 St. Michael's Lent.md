# St. Michael’s Lent Devotional & Fasting Plan

## August 15 – Feast of the Assumption (Holy Day of Obligation)
- **Fasting Discipline:** Feast (no fast)  
- **Special Devotion:** [[Consecration to Our Lady]] & [[Consecration to St. Michael|St. Michael]]  
- **Reading:** Luke 1:26–56  

---

## August 16–21 – Week 1: Orientation
- **Fasting Discipline:** Fast daily; Wed & Fri: abstinence from meat)  
- **Special Devotion:** [[Litany of Loreto]] and [[Prayer to St. Michael]] daily  
- **Reading:** Luke 1:26–56  

---

## August 22–28 – Week 2: Purification
- **Fasting Discipline:** Fast daily; Wed (no meat), Fri (bread & water if able)  
- **Special Devotion:** Psalm 51 and [[Prayer to St. Michael]] daily  
- **Reading:** Psalm 51  

---

## August 29–September 4 – Week 3: Spiritual Warfare
- **Fasting Discipline:** Fast daily; Wed & Fri (bread & water if able)  
- **Special Devotion:** [[Prayer to St. Michael]] AM & PM  
- **Reading:** Ephesians 6:10–20  

---

## September 5–11 – Week 4: Perseverance
- **Fasting Discipline:** Fast; Wed & Fri (bread & water if able)  
- **Special Devotion:** One specific act of charity and [[Prayer to St. Michael]] daily  
- **Reading:** 2 Timothy 4:1–8

---

## September 12–18 – Week 5: Protection
- **Fasting Discipline:** Fast daily; Wed & Fri (bread & water if able)  
- **Special Devotion:** Sprinkle holy water in home each night and [[Prayer to St. Michael]] daily
- **Reading:** Psalm 91  

---

## September 19–25 – Week 6: Final Preparation
- **Fasting Discipline:** Fast daily; Wed & Fri bread & water if able  
- **Special Devotion:** Confession & [[Litany of St. Michael]]  
- **Reading:** 1 Peter 5:6–11

---

## September 26–28 – Vigil Days
- **Fasting Discipline:** Fast, bread & water if able. 
- **Special Devotion:** [[Chaplet of St. Michael]] daily
- **Reading:** Revelation 12:7–12  

---

## September 29 – Feast of St. Michael
- **Fasting Discipline:** Feast (no fast)  
- **Special Devotion:** Renew consecration & festive meal  
- **Reading:** Daniel 10:10–21; Revelation 12:7–12
